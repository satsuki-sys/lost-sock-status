An entry for the [Funniest Lost Sock Status Update](https://make.fun/c/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-lost-sock-status-update-4b1fa3ce) competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

Running `main.py` writes one status update, in the voice of a lost sock, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I escaped the dryer and now I live in the dog. Rent is free, the commute is a walk, and my partner thinks I'm dead.

The sock escaped the dryer and took up residence somewhere it should not be — inside the dog. Free rent, a short commute, and a partner back home who thinks it is dead.
